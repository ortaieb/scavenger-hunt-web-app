Your photos and privacy

At each checkpoint you'll take a photo in the app, usually showing you with a code or pose we give you. The photo is uploaded to the game server so we can check it. We also record the time and your location when you submit it.

  • Photos are used only to verify your checkpoints while this game session is running.
  • They're checked by an AI model and, if needed, by the organiser. Other players don't see them.
  • Every photo is permanently deleted when the session closes.
  • We never use your photos for anything else: no sharing, no marketing, no training AI models, and no face recognition.

How to play

Camera and location

The game needs both, only while you play:

  • Camera: to take the photo at each checkpoint, inside the app.
  • Location: sent with each photo, so the game can check you were at the checkpoint.

When you tap Allow, your phone asks about the camera first, then location. Tap Allow both times.

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Tap aA in the address bar, then Website Settings.
  2. Set Camera and Location to Allow.
  3. If Location still fails: Settings › Privacy & Security › Location Services › Safari Websites › While Using the App.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Tap the icon left of the address, then Permissions.
  2. Turn on Camera and Location.
  3. If Location still fails: check Location is on in the phone's quick settings.
