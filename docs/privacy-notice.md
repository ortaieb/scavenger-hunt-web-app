## Sign-up screen text

**Your photos and privacy**

At each checkpoint you'll take a photo in the app, usually showing you with a code or pose we give you. The photo is uploaded to the game server so we can check it. We also record the time and your location when you submit it.

- Photos are used only to verify your checkpoints while this game session is running.
- They're checked by an AI model and, if needed, by the organiser. Other players don't see them.
- Every photo is permanently deleted when the session closes.
- We never use your photos for anything else: no sharing, no marketing, no training AI models, and no face recognition.

## Full privacy notice

### Who we are

[Hunt name] is run by [your name] ("we"). We're responsible for your data. Contact: [email].

### What we collect to verify checkpoints

- The photos you take at each checkpoint
- The time and your device location when you submit each photo
- A digital fingerprint of each photo, used to spot the same photo being submitted twice

### How we use it

We use this data only to check that you reached each checkpoint during the game. That means checking the photo matches the challenge, was taken during the game and was taken near the right place.

An AI model checks each photo. The organiser reviews a photo when the result is unclear or you dispute it. We don't use face recognition and don't try to identify you from your photos.

### Who else handles it

[AI provider] analyses photos on our behalf and [does not keep them or use them for training]. [Hosting provider] stores them on our behalf. [If either is outside the UK: name the safeguard, e.g. UK adequacy regulations.]

Other players never see your photos, and we don't share them with anyone else.

### How long we keep it

We keep photos, locations and fingerprints only while the game session is running. They're permanently deleted when the session closes, and in any case no later than [24 hours] after its scheduled end.

[After the game we keep only your results, meaning which checkpoints you completed and when, with no photos or locations.]

### What we'll never do

Use your photos for anything other than verifying your checkpoints in this game. We don't publish them, use them for marketing, use them to train AI models, or share or sell them.

### Our legal basis

We rely on the consent you give at sign-up. You can withdraw it at any time by [how]. If you do, we'll delete your photos straight away, but you won't be able to complete further checkpoints.

### Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to delete it, at any time. If you're unhappy with how we've handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

### Other people

Please keep other people out of your photos unless they've agreed to be in them.
